# Impact & Extensibility — what success actually buys

Impact scaled to what each result genuinely supports, not to the blueprint's framing. The
honest headline: **the impact lives in connectomics + ML methodology, not in life extension or
mind uploading.** Each track yields a citable result about how connectome structure relates to
function under compression, alignment, and perturbation. None moves the needle on human
migration, and this document says so explicitly.

## Per-track impact (conditional on the vetting gates passing)

### T1 — structured engram-aware compression
- **If H1 holds:** a *compression law* for connectome-driven models — a predictive rule
  (tied to a graph quantity like hub-mass fraction or spectral gap) for how far structure +
  bit-depth can be pushed before behavior collapses.
- **Who cites it:** neuromorphic / edge deployment of connectome models; comp-neuro work on
  "how much of the wiring matters for function."
- **Likelihood:** highest chance of a clean positive among the core tracks.
- **Venue class:** solid ML or computational-neuroscience paper.

### T2 — functional-transport alignment
- **If H2 holds:** the first *functional* (not merely structural) benchmark for connectome
  alignment — reframes "these graphs match with distance X" into "this much function survives
  transport."
- **If H3 holds (more likely, arguably more valuable):** a measured demonstration that the
  *target wiring*, not alignment quality, is the ceiling — a pointed limit on the entire
  "align-and-write" premise.
- **Who cites it:** the brain-alignment / optimal-transport community; sets an evaluation
  standard.

### T3 — replay→latent compression frontier
- **If H1/H2 hold:** a bits-per-event floor for reconstructing replayed sequences + a
  capacity/interference curve.
- **Who cites it:** neural-data compression, BCI backup-store design.
- **Scope:** smallest of the three core tracks, but clean and self-contained.

### T4 — in-silico write-compiler (surrogate; negative result is the prize)
- **If an impossibility region exists:** a *principled* result that local plasticity rules
  cannot install a transported function past a divergence threshold even under idealized
  conditions — far stronger than "we didn't build it."
- **If partial success:** bounds exactly what local-rule installation can and cannot do.
- **Who cites it:** plasticity theory, neuromorphic learning, and anyone evaluating
  "write-to-brain" proposals.

### T5 — partition separability (surrogate; refutation is the likely prize)
- **If H2 holds (most likely):** a measured refutation of the blueprint's foundational
  Base-OS/payload split, generalized into a reusable *separability index for connectomes*.
- **Who cites it:** network neuroscience; anyone making modularity/dissociation claims.

## Aggregate impact

- **Most probable real outcome:** 2–3 standalone methods/boundary papers (T1 strongest, T2
  second, T3 smallest) + 2 surrogate studies (T4, T5) mapping where the migration idea breaks.
- **The unifying thread:** across all five, the deliverable is *a quantitative map of the
  relationship between connectome structure and preserved function* under the three operations
  the blueprint needs — compress, transport, write/partition. That map is novel and useful
  regardless of migration, and it is the real contribution to claim.
- **What success does NOT buy:** no progress on non-destructive synaptic reading, cross-subject
  identity transfer, life extension, or uploading. Those stay in the Tier-A *absent* column.
  T4 and T5 most likely *tighten the evidence that the end-to-end vision is infeasible* — itself
  a contribution, just not the one the blueprint wanted.

## Honest probability read

| Track | Likely result | Impact if it lands |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Positive or clear boundary | Medium-high (compression law) |
| T2 | Corrective (H3) ≥ confirmation | Medium-high (new eval + limit) |
| T3 | Positive floor | Medium (information-theoretic) |
| T4 | Impossibility region *or* partial | High if sharp; low if muddy (gate catches muddy early) |
| T5 | Refutation (H2) | Medium-high (kills an untested premise) |

Expected value of the whole program: *a few genuine contributions plus several well-evidenced
negative results* — not "five confirmations." The vetting gates are set so dead hypotheses die
cheaply (e.g. T1-V2: if engram-aware ≈ uniform, stop).

## Extensibility (what each result unlocks next)

- **T1 → T4:** a validated compression metric gives T4 a principled "how much to install"
  target and a fidelity yardstick.
- **T5 → T2 → T4:** the surrogate chain composes into an end-to-end *in-silico* "partition →
  align → install" pipeline on C. elegans — the closest honest analogue to the blueprint,
  runnable on a laptop, with every link falsifiable.
- **Cross-substrate generalization:** worm → fly sub-circuit → mouse column is a built-in
  external-validity ladder; a result that holds across all three is far stronger.
- **Toward the goal:** see `IMPACT-STRATEGY.md` for how these assets are sequenced into a
  realistic, stubborn push on the actual end goal rather than five disconnected papers.
